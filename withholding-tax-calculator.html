Tax Guide

Filer vs Non-Filer

Filers are taxpayers on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) who file regular tax returns. Non-filers face higher tax rates to encourage compliance.

Rental Income Tax

Withholding tax is deducted at source on rental payments. Rates vary based on annual rental income and taxpayer status.

Property Transactions

Advance tax applies to property sales and purchases. Rates depend on property value and taxpayer status.

Tax Compliance

Maintain proper records and file returns on time. Withholding tax can be adjusted against annual tax liability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is withholding tax on property income?

Withholding tax is advance income tax deducted at source on rental income and property transactions. It's collected by the payer and deposited with FBR on behalf of the taxpayer.

How are tax rates different for filers and non-filers?

Filers (taxpayers on ATL) pay lower rates, while non-filers face higher rates. This encourages tax compliance and regular filing of returns.

Can withholding tax be adjusted in annual returns?

Yes, withholding tax can be adjusted against your annual tax liability when filing income tax returns. Excess tax paid can be claimed as refund.

What records should I maintain for property income?

Keep rental agreements, receipts, withholding tax certificates, property documents, and maintenance expense records for proper tax compliance.
