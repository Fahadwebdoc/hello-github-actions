## Pakistani Tax Context (2024-2025)

### Filer vs Non-Filer System
Pakistan operates a significant distinction between tax "filers" and "non-filers":
- **Filers:** Citizens who submit annual tax returns, receive preferential tax rates
- **Non-filers:** Face double taxation rates and restrictions on major purchases

### Key Tax Brackets for Salaried Individuals (2024-2025)
1. **Up to Rs. 600,000:** Tax-free
2. **Rs. 600,001 - Rs. 1,200,000:** 5% on excess
3. **Rs. 1,200,001 - Rs. 2,200,000:** Rs. 30,000 + 15% on excess
4. **Rs. 2,200,001 - Rs. 3,200,000:** Rs. 180,000 + 25% on excess
5. **Rs. 3,200,001 - Rs. 4,100,000:** Rs. 430,000 + 30% on excess
6. **Above Rs. 4,100,000:** Rs. 700,000 + 35% on excess

### Penalties for Non-Filers
- Cannot purchase property over Rs. 5 million
- Cannot buy new vehicles
- Subject to higher withholding tax rates (often double)
- Excluded from Active Taxpayer List (ATL)
